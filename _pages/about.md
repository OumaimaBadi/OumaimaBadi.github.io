I am **Oumaima Badi**, a PhD student in Computer Science working on **photorealistic human motion and style transfer for improving sports training**.

My research focuses on **photorealistic rendering**, **motion transfer**, and **3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)** for dynamic human scenes. I am particularly interested in building reliable and reproducible pipelines for training, rendering, and evaluating human avatar models.

Before starting my PhD at **INSA Rennes & DIGISPORT**, I worked on **deep learning for time series classification** during my research internship at **IRIMAS (UHA)**, which led to a publication at **IEEE BigData 2024**. More recently, my work on compact and structured 3D avatar modeling contributed to **MISTA**, accepted at **ICASSP 2026**. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

My broader interests include:
- dynamic human modeling
- 3D vision and neural rendering
- tensor decompositions
- experimental evaluation and reproducibility
- machine learning for real-world applications
